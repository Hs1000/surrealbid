SurrealBid is an independent, artist-first auction house for original work that does not fit neatly into a category.

A note on why we exist

The work that changes your perspective is rarely the work you expected to find.

We started SurrealBid for the moments when art interrupts the familiar: when an image is strange enough to make you pause, but precise enough to stay with you.

We believe artists making work at those edges deserve more than a crowded feed. And collectors deserve more than an endless catalogue. So we built a smaller, more intentional place for the two to meet.

— The SurrealBid team

Terms of sale.

This is a starting draft for the MVP and must be reviewed by a lawyer before SurrealBid takes real payments.

1. Who we are

SurrealBid is an online marketplace where independent artists auction original artwork to collectors. SurrealBid is not the seller of the artwork; each artist sells their own work.

2. Accounts

You need an account with a confirmed email address to bid or sell. You’re responsible for activity on your account and for keeping your password private.

3. Bidding

A bid is a binding offer to buy the artwork at that price if you are the highest bidder when the auction closes. Bids can’t be withdrawn. We may cancel bids or auctions that we reasonably believe are fraudulent or break these terms.

4. Winning and payment

When an auction closes, the highest bidder is emailed a link to pay. Payment is due within 3 days. If you don’t pay, we may offer the work to another bidder and restrict your account.

5. Artists

Artists must be approved before listing. By listing, an artist confirms they created the work, own the rights to sell it, and that the description and images are accurate. Artists ship the work to the buyer within 7 days of payment.

6. Prohibited content

No stolen, copied or misattributed work, no illegal content, and no shill bidding (bidding on your own work or arranging others to do so).

7. Fees, refunds and disputes

See fees and refunds. Contact us first if something goes wrong and we’ll try to resolve it.

Privacy policy.

Starting draft — have a lawyer review it, including against India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, before launch.

What we collect

Your name, email address and password (stored as a one-way hash). If you bid, your bids. If you apply as an artist, your application details, portrait and portfolio images. If you buy, your delivery name, phone and address. Payment card details are handled by Razorpay and never reach our servers.

How we use it

To run your account, process auctions and payments, review artist applications, send emails you need (confirmations, outbid notices, wins, receipts), and prevent fraud.

What’s public

Artwork listings, artist names, and bids. Bidders appear by first name and last initial only. Emails and addresses are never shown publicly.

Who we share it with

The artist you buy from receives your delivery details. Razorpay processes payments. Our hosting and email providers process data on our behalf. We don’t sell personal data.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by contacting us. Some records (such as completed sales) may need to be kept for legal and tax reasons.
